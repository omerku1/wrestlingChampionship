# Wrestling Betting League Scoring System

This document outlines the official scoring rules for the professional wrestling fantasy betting league. The system is designed to reward accurate predictions while introducing strategic depth through multipliers and performance-based guesses.

## 1. Match Betting
Each match on the card is assigned a **Base Score** (e.g., 1, 2, 3, or 5 points) based on its importance or predictability. Players must choose a winner for every match.

### Multipliers
Players can apply specific multipliers to a limited number of matches to boost their potential points. However, these multipliers also increase the risk.

* **Triple Multiplier (x3)**
    * **Correct Prediction:** Base Score × 3
    * **Incorrect Prediction:** Base Score × -3
* **Double Multiplier (x2)**
    * **Correct Prediction:** Base Score × 2
    * **Incorrect Prediction:** Base Score × -2
* **Joker Multiplier**
    * **Correct Prediction:** Base Score × 2
    * **Incorrect Prediction:** Base Score × -1

Matches without a multiplier follow standard scoring: **+Base Score** for a win and **0** for a loss.

## 2. Individual Categories & Event Guesses
Beyond match winners, players can earn or lose points based on specific event outcomes and superlatives.

### Category Points
For the following categories, players receive **+1 point** for a correct guess and **-1 point** for an incorrect guess:
* **Match of the Night (Best Match)**
* **Worst Match of the Night**
* **Longest Match (Duration)**
* **Shortest Match (Duration)**
* **Number of Title Changes**
* **Clean Finish Guarantee:** Guessed whether any match would end in a non-clean finish (DQ, Count-out, etc.).

### Text-Based Guess Penalties
For open-ended questions (e.g., "Who will be the mystery partner?" or "Will there be a surprise return?"), the scoring is strictly based on the number of incorrect names or theories provided:

* **Penalty Rule:** For every incorrect person, return, or turn guessed, the player receives **-1 point**.
* **Multiple Guesses:** If a player lists multiple options (e.g., "Cena, The Rock, or Stone Cold") and none occur, they receive a cumulative penalty (e.g., -3 points).
* **Success:** A correct guess in these categories avoids the penalty and may award bonus points as determined by the administrator.

## 3. Total Score Calculation
A player's total event score is the sum of:
1.  All match betting results (including multipliers).
2.  All individual category points.
3.  All accumulated text-based guess penalties.

The results are then aggregated into the **Global Leaderboard** to track performance across the entire season.
